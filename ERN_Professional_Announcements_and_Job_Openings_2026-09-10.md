## 1. 16th Workshop on Exchange Rates

**Type:** Call for Papers & Participants — Conference  
**Conference dates:** December 10–11, 2026  
**Location:** Hong Kong  
**Submission deadline:** September 13, 2026  
**Notification:** Late September 2026

### Description

The Hong Kong Institute for Monetary and Financial Research (HKIMR) will host the 16th Workshop on “Exchange Rates” on December 10–11, 2026 in Hong Kong, jointly organised with Banka Slovenije, Banca d’Italia, the Bank for International Settlements (BIS), the European Central Bank (ECB), and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA).

The workshop aims to provide a forum for discussing innovative research on analytical and policy issues related to FX markets, financial intermediation, and macrofinancial stability. Economists working in central banks, academia, or the private sector are welcome to participate and submit papers.

**Keynote speaker:** Refet Gürkaynak (Bilkent University and CEPR)

### Relevant Topics

- The drivers of exchange rates and related asset prices from a financial perspective, including the role of intermediaries
- Exchange rates and the transmission of monetary policy
- FX interventions and supply-side shocks, such as tariff policies and commodity prices
- Exchange rates and macrofinancial stability frameworks
- FX risk premia and their drivers
- Global linkages between capital flows, foreign exchange rates, and risk appetite
- The international use of currencies and its implications for exchange rates
- Implications of digital and tokenised assets for FX markets

### Submission Process

The workshop welcomes theoretical and empirical submissions. Interested authors should submit a paper by email to **hkimr@hkma.gov.hk** with the subject line **“Submission FX Workshop 2026”** by **September 13, 2026**.

Only completed papers will be considered because of the small number of papers the workshop can accept.

### Registration and Costs

- Registration fee: **None**
- Travel and accommodation: **Participants' responsibility**

- Fabio Fornari — ECB
- Matjaz Maletic — Banka Slovenije
- Andreas Schrimpf — BIS
- Giorgio Valente — HKIMR
- Philip Wooldridge — BIS
- Sonya Zhu — BIS

## 2. Call for Papers — EBRD Emerging Markets Group Workshop: “The Drivers of growth in the 21st century”

**Type:** Call for Papers & Participants — Conference  
**Workshop dates:** June 9–10, 2027  
**Location:** Bayes Business School, City St. George's, University of London — London  
**Submission deadline:** February 7, 2027  
**Notification:** End of March 2027

### Description

The Emerging Markets Group and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD) are organising a workshop on **“The Drivers of growth in the 21st century.”**

The workshop considers the slowdown in productivity growth in the 21st century, rapid population ageing, geopolitical tensions reshaping global trade and investment, the resurgence of industrial policy, climate and pollution risks, and the growing role of artificial intelligence. It also considers the implications for convergence of emerging market economies toward G7 income levels.

### Topics

Submissions are invited in areas including:

- Impact of globalization in an era of rising geopolitical tensions: changing geography of global connections and finance
- Advances in artificial intelligence: impact on the global financial system and allocation of resources in open economies
- Impact of financial development, including cryptocurrencies, on growth
- Demographic trends, finance, and growth
- The growing role of China in the global financial system
- Changes in economies’ comparative advantages over time: determinants and policies

Contributions in other areas, including public health, institutions, climate change, and inequality, are also welcome, particularly where they emphasise international finance and monetary policy.

### Keynote Speakers

- **Professor Javier Miranda** — Friedrich Schiller University Jena; Head of the Research Group Entrepreneurship, Innovation, and Productivity Growth; Halle Institute for Economic Research (IWH), Member of the Leibniz Association
- **Roman Arjona-Gracia** — European Commission, Chief Economist, DG GROW

### Submission Process

Completed papers in PDF form should be submitted through the following Qualtrics form:

<https://cityunilondon.eu.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3h3UPgbdd9TSOqO>

**Deadline:** February 7, 2027

Authors of accepted papers will be notified by the end of March 2027.

### Publication Possibility

Papers presented at the conference will be considered for publication in a special issue of the **Journal of International Money and Finance**, with guest editors **Kate Phylaktis** and **Alexander Plekhanov**, and co-editor **Menzie David Chinn**.

Submissions to the special issue will be subject to the journal's normal review process.

### Costs

Participants are expected to cover their own registration, travel, and accommodation costs.

### Workshop Organisers

- Kate Phylaktis — EMG, Bayes Business School
- Alexander Plekhanov — EBRD
- Meng Su — EBRD

### Scientific Committee

- Kate Phylaktis — EMG
- Alexander Plekhanov — EBRD
- Federica Coelli — EBRD
- Ana-Maria Fuertes — EMG
- Matthieu Bussiere — Bank of France
- David Furceri — IMF
- Chiara Banti — University of Essex

## 3. Call for Nominations and Self-Nominations — Director at the Max Planck Institute for Tax Law and Public Finance

**Type:** Other Announcement  
**Location:** Munich, Germany  
**Deadline:** October 15, 2026, midnight CET

### Description

The Max Planck Society is planning to appoint new Directors to the **Max Planck Institute for Tax Law and Public Finance (MPI)** in Munich, Germany.

The Max Planck Society is an independent association financed by the Federal Republic of Germany and the Länder and is described in the announcement as a major German institution for foundational research.

### Expected Profile

Directors are expected to:

- Conduct innovative research in tax law or public economics/political economy
- Lead a research department at the Institute
- Engage in the **Max Planck Hub Fiscal and Social State**, a partnership with the neighbouring MPI for Social Law and Social Policy
- Demonstrate excellent research and successful leadership
- Develop a long-term, visionary strategy for their research
- Make a substantial contribution to the Institute’s mission
- Be open to collaboration within the Max Planck Society and with globally leading research organisations

### Diversity and Inclusion

The Max Planck Society states that it is committed to diversity and inclusion and particularly encourages nominations of women. The announcement notes that, in cases of equal qualification, women will receive preferential consideration unless legally relevant personal circumstances require otherwise.

### Nomination Process

Nominations and self-nominations should be sent to:

**nominations@tax.mpg.de**

A nomination should include:

- Candidate’s name and current affiliation
- A brief motivational statement of **no more than 200 words** explaining the reasons for nomination

The call is part of the Institute’s **scouting procedure**. A formal appointment process will follow. A selection of self-nominated or nominated candidates may be invited for a hearing by the appointment committee established by the Human Sciences Section of the Max Planck Society.

Nominations will be treated in strict confidence.

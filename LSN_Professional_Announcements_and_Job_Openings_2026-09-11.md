## Professional Announcement

### Call for Nominations and Self-Nominations — Director, Max Planck Institute for Tax Law and Public Finance

**Institution:** Max Planck Institute for Tax Law and Public Finance  
**Location:** Munich, Germany  
**Announcement type:** Director appointment / scouting procedure

#### Position and research scope

The Max Planck Society is planning to appoint new Directors to the Institute. Directors are expected to:

- Conduct innovative research in **tax law** or **public economics / political economy**.
- Lead a research department at the Institute.
- Participate in the **Max Planck Hub Fiscal and Social State**, a research partnership with the neighboring Max Planck Institute for Social Law and Social Policy.
- Demonstrate excellent research, successful leadership, long-term research vision, and the potential to make a substantial contribution to the Institute's mission.
- Collaborate with other institutes within the Max Planck Society and with globally leading research organizations.

#### Diversity and inclusion

The Max Planck Society states a strong commitment to diversity and inclusion and especially encourages nominations of women and candidates from groups underrepresented in leadership positions. The announcement states that, in cases of equal qualification, women will receive preferential consideration unless legally relevant interests apply.

#### How to nominate

**Submit to:** [nominations@tax.mpg.de](mailto:nominations@tax.mpg.de)

A nomination should include:

1. Candidate's name and current affiliation.
2. A brief motivational statement of **no more than 200 words** explaining the reasons for the nomination.

This is part of the Institute's **scouting procedure**. A formal appointment procedure will follow. Some nominated candidates may later be invited to a hearing by the appointment committee of the Human Sciences Section of the Max Planck Society.

**Confidentiality:** All nominations will be treated in the strictest confidence.

## Job Opening

### European University Institute — Max Weber Postdoctoral Fellowships 2027/28

**Institution:** European University Institute (EUI)  
**Location:** Florence, Italy  
**Programme:** Max Weber Postdoctoral Programme

The programme is described as a highly competitive, multidisciplinary postdoctoral programme across the **economics, history, law, and social and political sciences**.

#### Eligibility

Applicants must be **within five years of the award of their PhD**.

Preference is given to candidates who:

- Recently completed their doctorate;
- Have not previously held a postdoctoral position; and/or
- Are on the academic or research-oriented job market.

The programme accepts applicants from anywhere in the world.

#### Affiliation options

Applicants may seek affiliation with one of the EUI's four departments:

- Economics
- History
- Law
- Political and Social Sciences

Applicants may also apply for affiliation with:

- Robert Schuman Centre for Advanced Studies
- Florence School of Transnational Governance

The programme notes that approximately **40 new Max Weber Fellows** join the EUI each year.

#### Additional opportunities

Dedicated grants are available for applicants from **Greece, Japan, and Poland**, subject to the relevant grant conditions.

A suitable applicant may also apply for an additional year at the **Graduate Institute of International and Development Studies in Geneva** (one fellowship available).

Candidates applying to the Department of History may indicate interest in affiliation with the **Alcide De Gasperi Research Centre** at the EUI.

**Application deadline:** **16 October 2026, 14:00 CEST**  
**Exception:** Department of Economics

**Programme page:** https://www.eui.eu/apply?id=max-weber-fellowships

**Questions:** applyfellow@eui.eu

### European University Institute — Jean Monnet Fellowships 2027/28

**Institution:** Robert Schuman Centre for Advanced Studies, EUI  
**Location:** Florence, Italy

The Robert Schuman Centre offers up to **20 Jean Monnet Fellowships** for the 2027/28 academic year.

#### Eligibility

The programme is open to scholars who obtained their doctorate **more than five years before September 1, 2027**. Eligible applicants include:

- Postdoctoral researchers
- Tenure-track academics
- Scholars seeking a sabbatical at the Robert Schuman Centre

Applicants are selected on the basis of their **CV and research proposal**. The proposal should fit one of the Centre's main research themes, programmes, or projects.

**Application deadline:** **23 October 2026, 14:00 CEST**

**Programme information:** https://www.eui.eu/apply?id=jean-monnet-fellowships

**Research centre:** https://www.eui.eu/en/academic-units/robert-schuman-centre-for-advanced-studies

### Practical priority

The Max Planck nomination call is a **senior leadership appointment**, not a standard postdoctoral or early-career application. The EUI Max Weber Programme is the most directly relevant opportunity for researchers within five years of the PhD. The Jean Monnet Fellowship is aimed at scholars more than five years past the doctorate.

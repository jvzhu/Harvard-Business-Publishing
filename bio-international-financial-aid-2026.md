# BIO International Student Financial Aid — Fall 2026 Application Open

*Forwarded via DAGRS/EALC/SSEAS undergrad listservs, August 27, 2026*
*Originally distributed by: Sage Erbez-Benson, International Student Adviser, Berkeley International Office (BIO)*

---

## Key Details

- **Application window:** Now through **September 13, 2026**
- **Eligibility:** International students who have completed at least one academic year in their current visa status
- **Type of aid:** Limited, need-based assistance

## Available Grants

| Grant | Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| International Undergraduate Tuition & Fee Grant | Undergraduate | — |
| International Undergraduate Need & Merit-Based Scholarship | Undergraduate | Fall only |
| International Graduate Tuition & Fee Grant | Graduate | — |
| International Graduate Parent Grant | Graduate | — |

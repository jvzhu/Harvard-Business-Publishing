# Call for Papers: Water for Peace and Prosperity

**Resolving Conflicts and Reducing Poverty Through Water Governance**

*Distributed via SRPN (Sustainability Research & Policy Network), SSRN — September 10, 2026*

## Organizers

Asian Development Bank Institute (ADBI), in partnership with:
- International Centre for Water Hazard and Risk Management (ICHARM)
- Meiji University
- National Graduate Institute for Policy Studies (GRIPS)
- The University of Tokyo

## Overview

Seeking chapter proposals for two edited volumes exploring the role of water in advancing peace, reducing poverty, and promoting sustainable prosperity. Builds on and serves as reference material for the Disaster Management Program jointly implemented by ICHARM and GRIPS.

## Resulting Publications

- **Volume I:** *Water for Peace: Resolving Conflicts and Contributing Prosperity*
- **Volume II:** *Water for Poverty: Water Security and Pathways to Prosperity*
- Knowledge products and learning materials for capacity-building programs

## Suggested Themes

**Water and Peace**
- Water conflicts, cooperation, and peacebuilding
- Transboundary water governance, diplomacy, and international law
- Water security in fragile, conflict-affected, and climate-vulnerable contexts
- Institutional, policy, and governance approaches to conflict prevention/resolution
- Science, technology, and innovative approaches for water cooperation

**Water and Poverty**
- Water security, poverty reduction, and inclusive development
- Water supply, sanitation, livelihoods, and human well-being
- Water governance, financing, and institutional reforms
- Water infrastructure, disaster resilience, and economic prosperity
- Climate adaptation and resilience for vulnerable and underserved communities

## Submission Guidelines

- **Length:** 3,000–5,000 words (including references/footnotes, excluding figures/tables)
- **Deadline:** November 6, 2026
- Indicate preferred volume at submission (editorial team may reassign based on thematic fit)
- Must be original, not published or under review elsewhere
- Must comply with ADBI Submission Guidelines

## Process for Selected Papers

- Authors invited to present at a virtual conference hosted by ADBI, **January 2027**
- Corresponding author presents with an external reviewer present, and serves as discussant for another paper
- Revisions based on reviewer feedback
- After peer review, accepted papers included as chapters in the edited volume

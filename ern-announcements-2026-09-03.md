## Call for Papers & Participants — Conference

### 16th Workshop on Exchange Rates
**Dates:** December 10–11, 2026 · **Location:** Hong Kong

Hosted by the Hong Kong Institute for Monetary and Financial Research (HKIMR), jointly organized with Banka Slovenije, Banca d'Italia, the Bank for International Settlements, the European Central Bank, and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority. Forum for research on FX markets, financial intermediation, and macrofinancial stability. Open to economists in central banks, academia, and the private sector.

**Keynote:** Refet Gürkaynak (Bilkent University and CEPR)

**Topics:**
- Drivers of exchange rates and related asset prices (incl. role of intermediaries)
- Exchange rates and monetary policy transmission
- FX interventions and supply-side shocks (tariffs, commodity prices)
- Exchange rates and macrofinancial stability frameworks
- FX risk premia and drivers
- Global linkages between capital flows, FX rates, and risk appetite
- International use of currencies
- Digital/tokenised assets and FX markets

**Submission:** Email completed papers to hkimr@hkma.gov.hk, subject "Submission FX Workshop 2026," by **September 13, 2026**. Notifications by late September. No registration fee; participants cover own travel/accommodation.

**Organising Committee:** Alessio Anzuini (Banca d'Italia), Egemen Eren (BIS), Fabio Fornari (ECB), Matjaz Maletic (Banka Slovenije), Andreas Schrimpf (BIS), Giorgio Valente (HKIMR), Philip Wooldridge (BIS), Sonya Zhu (BIS)

### 2026 NBS Conference on AI for Finance & NTU Academy of AI for Business and Social Science Research
**Conference:** Nov 20–21, 2026 · **Academy:** Nov 21–23, 2026 · **Location:** Nanyang Technological University, Singapore

Co-hosted by the Global Institute of Finance, Technology, and Society (GIFTS) and Nanyang Business School as part of the GIFTS-ADEFT Summit 2026, jointly with the inaugural AI and Big Data in Finance Research Forum (ABFR) Workshop.

**Key dates:**
- **September 20, 2026:** Deadline — conference/workshop paper submission and Academy application
- **October 15, 2026:** Acceptance/admission decisions

**1. NBS Conference on AI for Finance**
- Chairs: Xin (Simba) Chang, Lin William Cong (both NTU)
- Keynotes: Hui Chen (MIT), Sydney Ludvigson (NYU, Director, NBER Asset Pricing Program)
- Program committee includes scholars from Chicago Booth, Harvard, Penn, Cornell, UC Berkeley, Emory, HKU, EPFL, Stanford, Dartmouth, Arizona State, Johns Hopkins, USC, Washington University in St. Louis, and NTU.
- **Topics:** Generative AI & foundation models in finance; AI-driven asset pricing/portfolio management; ML for forecasting; alternative data/NLP; AI in banking & credit risk; fintech; human-AI collaboration; AI for climate finance/ESG; AI governance & ethics.
- **Submit:** https://event.ntu.edu.sg/call-for-papers-ai-for-finance-2026 — deadline September 20, 2026, 11:59 PM SGT.
- **ABFR Workshop** (2nd conference day, Nov 21): submit via https://www.abfr-forum.org/workshop-portal — deadline September 20, 2026 (AoE), no fee.
- **ABFR Doctoral Research Symposium** (hybrid; for PhD job-market candidates/junior faculty within 3 years of graduation): requires faculty nomination + paper. Faculty portal: https://www.abfr-forum.org/doctoral-symposium-faculty-portal; candidate portal: https://www.abfr-forum.org/doctoral-symposium-jm-portal. Deadline October 15, 2026 (AoE). Travel support up to SGD 1,500 for in-person attendance.
- **Dual submission:** "Fault-free" option with *Management Science* for select recommended papers (see journal's Policy for Dual Submissions Through Conferences).
- Questions: Pietro Bini, pbini@bu.edu

**2. NTU Academy of AI for Business and Social Science Research**
- Chairs: Hui Chen (MIT), Jun Yang (NTU)
- Lecturers: Pietro Bini (BU), Lin William Cong (NTU), Lawrence Jin (Cornell), Hannah Yee-Fen Lim (NTU), Yang Liu (NTU), Lea Stern (U. Washington)

- **Schedule:**

  - Day 2: "AI in Corporate Finance" (Stern); "Economic World Models and AI Tokenomics" (Cong)
  - Day 3: "Legal, Governance, Ethical Issues Concerning AI" (Lim); "From Brain to Intelligence: Agents in Business and Science" (Liu)
- **Apply:** https://event.ntu.edu.sg/ntu-academy-AI-2026 — deadline September 20, 2026, 11:59 PM SGT; decisions by October 15, 2026.

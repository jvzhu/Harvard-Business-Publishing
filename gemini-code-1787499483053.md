#### 📝 About
**Vivien Jiaqian Zhu (朱嘉倩)** earned an A.B. (distinction) in Comparative Literature and Chinese Literature, with a minor in History of Art from UC Berkeley (2018). She was a recipient of the Theresa Hak Kyung Cha Award in Comparative Literature, and a Regent's Scholar by the Graduate Division at the University of California, Berkeley to pursue a Ph.D. degree. Her dissertation investigates the expression of self, interior space/interiority, and architectural tropes in literature and art.

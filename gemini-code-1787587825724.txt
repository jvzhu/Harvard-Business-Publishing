\begin{abstract}
\noindent This chapter introduces the ``corporate stage'' framework to examine how algorithmic surveillance and hybrid work reconfigure labor visibility, self-perception, and spectatorship. Synthesizing Goffman's dramaturgy, Foucault's panopticism, and Zuboff's surveillance capitalism, we analyze how domestic space is converted into a conditional front stage, forcing workers into dual roles as performing actors and self-monitoring audiences.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} Algorithmic Surveillance, Dramaturgy, Hybrid Work, Panopticism, Surveillance Capitalism, Spectatorship

## Citation lead for "Corporate Stage" chapter

**Valentine, M. A., Pratt, A. L., Hinds, R., & Bernstein, M. S. (2024).** "The Algorithm and the Org Chart: How Algorithms Can Conflict with Organizational Structures." *Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction*, 8, 1–31. DOI: [10.1145/3686903](https://doi.org/10.1145/3686903)

**Summary:** A 10-month ethnographic study of a large online retail company showing that organizational structures supporting effective human decision-making can conflict with those needed for algorithmic decision-making — suggesting org charts themselves may need to evolve to reflect algorithmic behavior as algorithms become embedded in workplaces.

**Relevance:** Complements the chapter's Goffman/Foucault/Zuboff synthesis by extending the argument from individual worker visibility to organizational-structure-level effects — algorithmic logic reshaping not just how workers are watched, but how companies structure themselves.

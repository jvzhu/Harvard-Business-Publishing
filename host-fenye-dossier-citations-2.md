## Dossier overview

- **Chen**, "Fenye by the Numbers" — the imperial court's earliest attempt to replace *fenye* with "Gnomonic Degrees" (晷度) following the spread of Western astronomical/geographical knowledge.
- **Wu**, "An Encounter of Incommensurables" — spread of Western Learning (*xixue* 西學) among local-level literati across the empire, 1660–1820.
- **Brown**, "From Fenye to Fengshui" — how court astrologers and popular geomancers across the metropole and Qing frontiers kept using *fenye* through the dynasty's close.
- **Zhang**, "From Fenye to Latitude and Longitude" — *fenye*'s gradual replacement by a latitude/longitude positioning system by the late 19th century.

## Knight-Hennessy Scholars Info Session (On-Campus)

- **Date:** Tuesday, September 1, 2026
- **Time:** 12:00–1:00 PM
- **Location:** 370 Dwinelle Hall
- **Registration:** Required — [apply.knight-hennessy.stanford.edu](https://apply.knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/register/?id=f1a4754e-c371-42fa-98e6-a9e370715ec7)

## Soros Fellowship for New Americans Info Session (Online)

- **Date:** Wednesday, September 2, 2026
- **Time:** 12:00–1:00 PM
- **Format:** Zoom webinar
- **Registration:** Required — [Zoom registration link](https://us02web.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_LOYvMRepSGGfxQ1LaQJvxg#/registration)

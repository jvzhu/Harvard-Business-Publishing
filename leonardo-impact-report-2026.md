# Leonardo's Impact: Explore Future Frontiers

*From: Diana Ayton-Shenker, CEO & President, Leonardo/ISAST*

---

## Summary

Leonardo/ISAST has released a new **Impact Report**, highlighting stories from across its programs and network on how creative inquiry drives connections, experimentation, and contributions at the intersection of art, science, and technology. The release comes as Leonardo approaches its **60th anniversary**.

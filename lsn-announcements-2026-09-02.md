## Call for Papers — Competitions

### ACCFSL – 2027 Writing Competition

The American College of Consumer Financial Services Lawyers (ACCFSL) is holding its 2027 annual writing competition recognizing contributions to U.S. consumer financial services law, in three categories:

| Category | Award |
|---|---|
| Books | $5,000 |
| Publishable articles, book chapters, or substantial book reviews (professional) | $3,500 |
| Publishable student case notes, articles, or comments | $1,500 |

Winners also receive a Certificate of Recognition. Awards may be made in all, some, or no categories depending on submissions. Official government-agency publications are not eligible.

**Eligibility:** Must address U.S. consumer financial services law. Topics principally about securities regulation, bankruptcy, insurance, or bank safety/soundness are excluded unless they bear directly on consumer financial services law.

**Timeline:** Entries must be written/published between November 1, 2025 and November 1, 2026. Submission deadline: **November 1, 2026**.

**Submission:** Electronic format; authors may submit directly or via a third party (with author consent and contact info). Unpublished entries should be typed, double-spaced, and conform to law review standards.

**Contact:**
- Lynette I. Hotchkiss, Chair, ACCFSL Annual Writing Competition — lynette.hotchkiss@lendingpad.com
- cc: jheckhaus@counselorlibrary.com
- More info: https://accfsl.org/writing-competition/

## Job Openings

### South Texas College of Law—Houston
**Tenure-Track or Visiting Assistant Professor**

STCL invites applications for full-time tenure-track positions and visiting assistant professor (VAP) positions beginning the 2027–28 academic year. VAP program runs ~2 years with mentoring and teaching support.

**Priority teaching areas:** Contracts, Commercial Law (Payments Systems, Secured Transactions, Sales), Legal Research & Writing, Evidence.

**About STCL:** Private, nonprofit, ABA-accredited, AALS member law school in downtown Houston, founded 1923 (oldest law school in the city). ~49 full-time professors, 1,188 full- and part-time students. Home to a nationally decorated advocacy program, the Frank Evans Center for Conflict Resolution, and the Transactional Practice Center.

**Apply:** Send letters of interest and resumes to Professor Joe Leahy, Faculty Appointments Committee Chair — jleahy@stcl.edu
**Website:** http://www.stcl.edu

### City University of Hong Kong
**Chair Professor / Professor / Associate Professor / Assistant Professor**

The School of Law seeks scholars in all areas of law, with particular interest in **Private International Law, Maritime Law, and Patent Law**, as part of a strategy to grow faculty size and research performance. Degree programs offered: LLB, JD, LLMArbDR, LLM, PCLL.

**Requirements:** PhD or equivalent; superior academic record with strong evidence/potential for research and teaching excellence; willingness to contribute to faculty and university life.

Recent PhDs from internationally renowned institutions with strong publication records may qualify for the **Presidential Assistant Professor Scheme** (details: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/vpti/paps/).

CityUHK is also broadly recruiting across many fields (biomedical science, computer/data science, AI/robotics, digital humanities, law and technology, etc.) tied to strategic themes: One Health, Digital Society, Smart City, Matter, Brain.

**Apply:** Online application + current CV. Nominations to Dean of School of Law — lin.feng@cityu.edu.hk

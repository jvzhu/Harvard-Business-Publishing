## Athabasca University
### Assistant/Associate Professor, Legal Studies

- **Location:** Alberta, Canada (Athabasca University, Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences)
- **Start date:** December 1, 2026
- **Two positions**, applicants must specify Position A or Position B in cover letter:
  - **Position A** — Assistant Professor. Research areas: commercial law, dispute resolution, Indigenous laws, jurisprudence, or legislative drafting.
  - **Position B** — Assistant or Associate Professor. Also serves as Program Director of the Graduate Diploma in Legislative Drafting (stipend + partial teaching release). Focus: legislative drafting, statutory interpretation, public law.
- **Qualifications:** PhD in Law, demonstrated teaching excellence, strong research record, success securing Tri-Agency or comparable external funding. Distance learning/digital teaching experience preferred.
- **Application requires:** cover letter, CV, research statement + outputs, teaching dossier (philosophy statement, evaluations if available, sample syllabus). Incomplete doctorates not reviewed.
- **Contact:** dhoang@athabascau.ca
- **Link:** [Application portal](https://athabascau.acquiretm.com/job_details_clean.aspx?id=3260)

## UC Law San Francisco

- **Role:** Chief executive officer of the College — academic affairs, finance, fundraising, government relations, strategic partnerships, enrollment, operations, and the Academic Village campus development.
- **Budget managed:** $152 million
- **Salary range:** $522,200–$607,500
- **Key priorities:** long-term financial sustainability, advancing the Academic Village (Tower at 100 McAllister renovation, completion fall 2027), strengthening visibility/reputation, faculty recruitment/retention.
- **Deadline:** Materials preferred by **September 21, 2026**
- **Submit to:** Joi Hayes-Scott, Russell Reynolds Associates — UCLawSF.Chancellor@russellreynolds.com (CV + letter of interest, PDF preferred)
- **Full position spec:** [bit.ly/UCLawSFChancellor](https://bit.ly/UCLawSFChancellor)

## University of Alabama
### Assistant, Associate, or Full Professor of Legal Writing

- **Position type:** 9-month, non-tenure, contract-based (initial 3-year term, presumptively renewable thereafter)
- **Teaching load:** Two first-year legal writing sections; develop and teach an upper-level writing/drafting course twice over the contract term
- **Optional administrative roles:** Director of Legal Writing Program and/or Director of Moot Court Program (reduced teaching load, added responsibilities)
- **Qualifications:** JD from ABA-accredited school, strong academic record, effective legal writing skills, active state bar membership in good standing
- **Application:**
  - Entry-level (Assistant): [Job #529994](http://careers.ua.edu/cw/en-us/job/529994)
  - Lateral (Associate/Full): [Job #529853](http://careers.ua.edu/cw/en-us/job/529853)
  - Materials: resume, official transcript, cover letter, three references, writing sample
- **Deadline:** Open until filled
- **Contact:** Kimberly Boone, Chair, Legal Writing Faculty Appointments Committee — kboone@law.ua.edu

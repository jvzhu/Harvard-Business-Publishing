## Authors

Pavel Petrov, Vassil Milev, Filipp Safronov, Vladimir Filatov

## Abstract

This paper explores the engineering principles of role-based AI translation systems. A configurable experimental framework was developed based on a review of existing multi-agent translation architectures to evaluate role allocation, model selection, glossary integration, and review strategies. Controlled experiments have identified the key factors that influence translation quality, and have formulated practical design principles for the development of efficient, domain-specific Role-Based AI translation systems.
